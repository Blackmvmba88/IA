# Technical sources

These sources establish the physical ideas used by the reduced-order models.
They do not supply proprietary geometry or production dimensions.

## Breguet — Principles of the Tourbillon
https://www.breguet.com/en/magazine-quai-de-l-horloge/272465/principles-tourbillon

Useful points:
- ideal hairspring restoring torque is proportional to angular displacement;
- balance + hairspring form the watch oscillator;
- real center-of-gravity displacement introduces gravity-dependent disturbing torques;
- tourbillon energy must sustain both the oscillator/escapement and cage rotation.

## Jaeger-LeCoultre — regulating organ / hairspring
https://www.jaeger-lecoultre.com/in-en/watches/master-grande-tradition/master-grande-tradition-calibre-985/q5246580

Useful points:
- balance wheel, hairspring and escapement form the regulating organ;
- hairspring shape and attachment affect breathing/concentricity;
- amplitude and positional behavior are separate engineering concerns.

## Breguet — high frequency 10 Hz
https://www.breguet.com/en/breguet-house/1999-present/introduction-high-frequency-10hz

Useful point:
- higher oscillator frequency changes energy/material demands; frequency is therefore not a free optimization variable.
